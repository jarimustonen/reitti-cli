# Code of Conduct

## Expected conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Critique ideas and changes rather than people, accept correction, and take responsibility for mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Moderation

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content; limit participation; or block a participant when needed to protect the community. Moderation will be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

For abuse occurring on GitHub, use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** action on the relevant comment, issue, pull request, or account. This sends the report through GitHub's established abuse-handling process. Do not repost harassment or personal information in a new public issue.

The project does not publish a separate conduct-reporting address. For concerns that GitHub's reporting tools do not cover, contributors with an existing private collaboration channel may contact a repository administrator there without reposting sensitive details.
